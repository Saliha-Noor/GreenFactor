Publication Draft Excerpt

"Our empirical evaluation of 95 refactoring cases across 69 real-world open-source repositories reveals that energy-aware refactoring is most impactful in high-level interpreted runtimes. Interpreted-JIT runtimes achieved a mean energy reduction of 17.1% (RQ1), primarily driven by the cache_reuse pattern (RQ2), which eliminates redundant dynamic dispatch and evaluation. In contrast, compiled-native runtimes yielded modest gains (mean savings of 4.4%), as low-level compilers (GCC, Clang) already optimize loop-invariant code at compile time. Statistical significance testing (p < 0.05) confirmed that green refactoring yields measurable energy reductions in 18.9% of production workloads with moderate-to-large effect sizes (d up to 0.88)."
